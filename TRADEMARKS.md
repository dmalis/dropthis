# Trademark policy

> Draft — wording not yet reviewed by counsel.

Apache-2.0 grants rights to the code, not to the dropthis name or logo.

- You may truthfully state that a product or service is built on or compatible with dropthis.
- You may not call a modified build or a hosted service "dropthis", use its logo, or imply
  endorsement, without written permission.
- Unmodified builds distributed under the project's own release artifacts may keep the name.
